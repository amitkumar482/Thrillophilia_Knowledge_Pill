Vehicle Operations & Driver Guidelines

Driver Operating Window: 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM

Drivers are available between 7:00 AM and 8:00 PM. Additional usage beyond 8:00 PM incurs direct customer-paid driver allowance surcharges.

  • Airport Alignment Check: Verify whether pickup/drop is at Kochi (COK) or Trivandrum (TRV) airport.
  • Sequential Driving Flow: Ensure circuit flows logically (e.g. COK ➔ Munnar ➔ Thekkady ➔ Alleppey ➔ TRV/COK).
  • Pre-Trip Briefing: Always communicate vehicle timing expectations to customers before closing.

Inclusions vs Exclusions Rules

Standard Inclusions
  • Airport pickup & drop transfers
  • Breakfast from Day 2 onwards
  • Private AC vehicle for itinerary
  • All meals on Houseboat stay
Standard Exclusions
  • Lunch and Dinner at hotels
  • Entry tickets (unless specified)
  • Driver late-night allowance
  • Personal expenses & tips

Air Routes & Flight Alignment Rules

Direct Flight Connectivity

Direct flights available from DEL, BOM, AMD, CCU, BLR, and HYD to Hanoi (HAN), Da Nang (DAD), and Ho Chi Minh City (SGN).

Arrival Flight Alignment Rule

Always check arrival flight times before fixing first-day tours to avoid customer exhaustion or missed transfers.

Open-Jaw Routing Recommendation

Build open-jaw itineraries (e.g., Fly in HAN ➔ Fly out SGN) to avoid unnecessary internal travel backtracking.

Visa Policy & Transfer Mode Rules

Mainland E-Visa vs Phu Quoc Exemption

Mainland E-Visa: Mandatory for mainland entry. Online application takes 3–5 working days.

Phu Quoc 30-Day Exemption: Indian passport holders receive 30 days Visa-Free entry when flying directly into Phu Quoc Island.

Transfer Mode Distinction

Private Transfers: Recommended for families & luxury clients for maximum comfort.

Seat-In-Coach (SIC): Budget-friendly; requires strict adherence to pick-up schedules.

Golden Rule for Tickets

Theme Park & Cable Car Ticket Inclusions

Major attraction tickets (Ba Na Hills Golden Bridge Cable Car, Fansipan Cable Car, VinWonders, VinSafari) are included ONLY when explicitly listed in the Quotation Builder. Never promise optional theme park tickets in base quotes without verification!
